Suggested development order
1. Make availability and status handling reliable. Add transactional conflict protection for concurrent bookings, validate room/bed capacity and booking invariants consistently, and add deliberate status transitions for tentative, confirmed, checked in, checked out, cancelled, and no-show. This is the foundation for trustworthy operations.
2. Make availability visible at the right level. Show room and bed occupancy separately, display booking status accurately, and expose occupied capacity for each date. Add filters for room type/status and a clear way to move between months.
3. Complete the staff booking workflow. Add booking references and creator attribution on creation; support cancellation with reason and timestamp; provide edit, status, and cancellation actions; and display booking history or audit events.
4. Add payment tracking. Provide a booking balance summary, record payments and refunds with method and comment, and show outstanding amounts. Keep payment records separate from the booking price.
5. Improve rates and quotes. Show a price breakdown by night and surcharge, make manual price overrides explicit and auditable, and validate that relevant active rate plans are configured before staff can confirm a booking.
6. Add operational reporting. Start with occupancy, arrivals/departures, revenue collected, outstanding balances, and booking sources. Define revenue based on actual payment entries, not just booking totals.
7. Consider guest-facing booking later. Once availability, pricing, payments, and confirmations are dependable, add a guest booking and confirmation flow, then consider channel integrations if the business needs them.
